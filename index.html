Voiceprints are saved only on this phone. Tap ✓ next to a guessed name to confirm it, or tap the name to correct it — each confirmation adds a sample (one per person per session). With more samples, names appear sooner: about 8 s of speech at 3 samples, 5 s at 5, 3 s at 8+.
